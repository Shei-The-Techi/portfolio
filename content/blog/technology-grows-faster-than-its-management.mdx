As teams grow, tools get added, suppliers own different parts of the environment and workarounds become routine. Staff may not know which system holds the current information, who can approve a change or whether an external provider has finished a task. The result is often **lost time, unclear accountability and decisions made without a complete picture**.

## Recurring issues

The same faults return because the cause, fix or procedure is not clearly recorded.

## Unclear ownership

Access, subscriptions, system decisions or supplier actions fall between people.

## Manual workarounds

Duplicated entry and informal handoffs make work harder to track and maintain.

## The aim

The aim is not to add more technology. It is to make the technology already in use easier to understand, support and manage.
