An IT and systems health check is for a business that is unsure what it relies on, who owns it or which gaps deserve attention first.

## What I review

- Agreed systems, accounts and key owners
- Access and permissions, supplier dependencies and subscriptions
- Available documentation, devices or platforms, and current backup arrangements

## What you receive

- Findings report and prioritised action plan
- Ownership and dependency list
- Management discussion to agree what to address next

## A simple inventory

An ownership and dependency list can be as light as a spreadsheet. This is an illustrative example of the columns:

```csv
system,owner,access_reviewed,backup_in_place,open_actions
Email,Operations lead,yes,yes,0
Cloud storage,Operations lead,yes,yes,1
Website hosting,External developer,no,unknown,2
```

## Success measures

Success measures agreed at the start may include:

1. Critical systems with an identified owner
2. Accounts reviewed
3. Priority actions identified
4. Unresolved dependencies documented
